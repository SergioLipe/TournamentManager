Can't decide? Let a tournament do it.

Tournament turns any choice into a knockout bracket. Two competitors appear, you tap the one you like best, and the winner moves on, round after round, until only your champion is left.

• 24 ready-made themes: animals, dinosaurs, dog and cat breeds, food, desserts, cocktails, beers, films, TV shows, anime, video games, superheroes, rock bands, rap artists, footballers, historical figures, generals, paintings, world landmarks, European capitals, classic cars and aircraft
• Choose an 8-player or a 16-player bracket
• Play with your own list of 2 to 16 names, perfect for settling arguments with friends
• "Next match" keeps the tournament moving with one tap
• "Surprise me" picks a random theme for you
• Shared statistics: every finished tournament counts, so you can see which competitors win most across all players
• No account, no ads, no personal data

Perfect for when you can't make up your mind, for a friendly competition, or just to find out what you really like best.
